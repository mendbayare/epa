The laboratory ran a practical workshop on SCADA and operator interfaces,
attended by third-year students.

Participants built a simple control screen and completed an exercise reading
measurement data from laboratory equipment and displaying it. Signal naming,
screen structure and alarm configuration were discussed together.

At the end of the workshop each team presented its own screen and demonstrated
that it worked.
