The laboratory ran a theory seminar for first-year students. Its aim was to
apply concepts from lectures to concrete problems.

Students worked in small groups on circuit calculation problems and discussed
their results together. Teaching staff followed each group's work and
supported them through questions and answers.

The laboratory plans to repeat this format of seminar each semester.
