A student team from the laboratory took part in the engineering competition
held across the school.

The team prepared a small model of power supply monitoring and presented their
solution for collecting and visualising measurement data. The judges assessed
the practical application of the solution and its documentation.

The experience gained has been noted for use in preparing for next year.
