The laboratory held an internal seminar on power quality research.

Voltage fluctuation and harmonic indicators collected during the measurement
period were presented, and the influence of measurement conditions and
instrument settings was discussed. Participants compared the results against
the requirements of the standard.

Proposals to widen the study and add measurement points were noted.
