Students from the laboratory made a visit to a working substation, organised
with the support of specialists from the sector.

They were shown the protection panel, the control system and the placement of
communication equipment, and heard an explanation of the operating regime.
They were able to compare how the model equipment seen in the laboratory is
used under real conditions.

Teaching staff consider visits of this kind useful for connecting theoretical
knowledge with the working environment.
